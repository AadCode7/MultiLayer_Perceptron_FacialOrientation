# MultiLayer_Perceptron_FacialOrientation

Develop a SVM Classifier to determine the orientation from a part of the face. Extract a square region from the image and report whether it is the right way up, rotated 90-degree right, rotated 90-degree left or upside down. The square regions are of size 90, 50 or 30 pixels and are taken from original images of complete face that are 94 pixels wide by 125 pixels high.
